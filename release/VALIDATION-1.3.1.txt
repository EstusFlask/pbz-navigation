  above Reset. It is the hidden custom-color field's default swatch.

Root cause and minimal probe

  in entry.outer. Runtime collapses only entry.row on initial render/update.
- Probe: row visibility=1(collapsed), outer=0(visible), swatch=3(hit-test-invisible),
  sibling=true. Changing only outer visibility hides the swatch. Reopening Custom
  also requires the outer to be restored on update.
